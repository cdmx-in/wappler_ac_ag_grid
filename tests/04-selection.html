Status toggle column

Data has a status field, so row_status_event=true renders checkboxCellRenderer. The toggle must dispatch its event via a delegated listener and emit no inline onclick — inline handlers are blocked by a CSP without 'unsafe-inline' in script-src. The puppeteer runner reads window.__statusCheck.
